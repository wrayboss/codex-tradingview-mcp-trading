# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors should keep discussions professional, specific, and useful. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is backed by evidence and focused on improving the repository.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Keep feedback about code, tests, safety, and user impact.
- Report uncertainty clearly.
- Protect private data and trading account information.
- Avoid claims about returns, readiness, or safety that are not supported by evidence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private account details, private logs, or runtime artifacts.
- Encouraging users to bypass safety gates or place trades without explicit approval.
- Misrepresenting test results, profitability, or production readiness.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, block contributors, or decline changes that violate this code of conduct or weaken the repository's safety posture.
